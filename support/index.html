Frequently asked questions

Is Global Monetary Systems free?
Yes — every page's Essentials layer is free, permanently, with no account required. Global Monetary Systems Pro ($9.99/month) unlocks the deeper Applied and Research layers. See what Pro includes.

Does it work offline?
Yes. All 359 pages ship with the app. The only network activity is a data refresh you trigger, an opt-in auto-sync, citation pages you tap, and a one-time AI model download if you turn that feature on.

Where is my data stored?
On your device, and only there. There are no accounts and no cloud copies of anything. See the privacy policy for full detail.

How does the on-device AI work?
You choose a language model sized for your device and download it once; after that, every question and answer stays entirely on your phone. It answers grounded in the app's own sourced pages and names which pages it used.

How do I restore my Pro subscription?
Purchases are handled by the App Store or Google Play. On a new device, use the app's restore option, or reinstall while signed in to the same Apple/Google account.

Is this financial advice?
No. The app explains mechanisms and what has historically followed a given kind of event — it never tells you what to buy, sell, or invest in. See the disclaimer.
