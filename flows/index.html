How money actually moves.

Every major institution and system, on one map — then watch a real payment travel through it.

The one thing most explanations get wrong: SWIFT carries payment instructions, never money. A dollar or a euro only ever moves by debiting one account and crediting another, inside that currency's own real-time settlement system, in central-bank money. Why this matters

The map, not just the animation

A single payment only touches a handful of these nodes. The rest are the architecture that makes the whole system possible — worth understanding even when they're not on today's path.

📡

Messaging ≠ money

SWIFT is a secure messaging network linking over 11,000 banks. It's often mistaken for a payments system — it isn't one. Every settlement above happens somewhere else.

🌐

Every currency, one pattern

Dollars settle at the Fed via Fedwire. Euros settle at the ECB via TARGET2. Every other currency on earth follows the identical pattern with its own central bank and system.

🏢

The architecture above it

The IMF, World Bank, and BIS never touch an individual payment — they keep the system itself solvent, coordinated, and standardized so the pattern above keeps working.
