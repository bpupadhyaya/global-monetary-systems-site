Features

Built to be a working reference, not a news feed

For the general public, working professionals and policymakers, and graduate students and faculty — one body of content, never a simplified spin-off.

📈

Live official data

Exchange rates, policy rates, and inflation prints — refreshed on demand from the IMF, World Bank, BIS, ECB and national central banks. Keyless, official APIs only.

🔖

Every claim cited

Citations are tappable and open in-app at full width, 85% height, and return you to exactly where you were. Never a bare footnote.

🌐

History & the alternatives

Barter to the classical gold standard, Bretton Woods to the floating era — plus hawala, mobile money, community currencies, stablecoins, DeFi and CBDCs.

🧠

Ask a model, on your phone

An on-device AI answers questions grounded in the app's own sourced pages and names exactly which pages it used. Nothing you ask ever leaves your phone.
